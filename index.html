About

Design led, code backed.

I started out sketching app screens in notebooks and ended up somewhere more useful: shipping real products end to end. I care about the fifteen small decisions that make an interface feel obvious — spacing, motion, wording, the moment something loads.

These days I split my time between UX/UI design and frontend development, which means I get to design something in the morning and build it by the afternoon. No handoff friction, no lost intent.
